Plinorbit is a one-screen physics drop game. A glass piece falls through pins toward pockets; the player tilts the board, lights stars, and tries to land in an open pocket. It is for someone who wants a short, repeatable aim-and-drop session. Nothing is wagered.

No credentials or demo account are needed. A cold launch opens straight onto the playfield in a dark full-screen view. There is no title screen, sign-in, or first-run card. The top bar shows the chapter as "01", the score as "0", a pause control (VoiceOver name "Pause"), a thin progress track, and the line "Light every star. Tap drops, hold aims." The glass rests at the top of a pin field. Stars sit on the board. Five pockets line the bottom, and the two outer pockets are marked as hazards. Touch the board to tilt it toward the finger. Release the touch, including a short tap, to let the glass go. While it is falling, another hold does not aim or drop again. Each lit star adds 10 to the score. Landing in an open pocket after every star is lit adds 25, parks the glass at the top, and loads the next drop. Landing in an outer pocket, or in an open pocket before every star is lit, ends the run with no alert. The board returns to "01" and the score returns to "0". Two successful pockets advance the chapter. The chapter card is not shown for chapter 1, including a card titled "Glass Lip". It appears only after the player has cleared enough pockets to enter a later chapter. The board freezes under the card until "Continue" is tapped. Chapter 2 is titled "Warm Pins", with the line "A full tilt can miss the pocket. Keep the board level when the lane is open." Chapter 3 is titled "Pale Weather", with the line "An untilted bounce still clears a peg. Spend the tilt only to steer." Chapter 4 is titled "Last Pocket", with the line "Light every star, then take the center pocket." Further landings after "04" stay on "04". Tap the pause control to open "Paused", caption "The dome holds." "Resume" hides the overlay and continues the same drop. "Records" opens the Records sheet, and "Settings" opens the Settings sheet. The drop stays paused underneath. "Continue" and "Resume" are the only ways back into a frozen drop. Records is titled "Records" and "Close" dismisses it. The six rows are "Best score", "Highest chapter", "Chapter 1 clears", "Chapter 2 clears", "Chapter 3 clears", and "Chapter 4 clears". Before any finished drop those numbers read 0, 1, 0, 0, 0, and 0. Rows are not tappable. Settings is titled "Settings" and "Close" dismisses it. It shows "ORBITGLASS", "On this device", and "Best score and chapter stay here. Nothing is wagered." "Contact" opens the support page in the browser. "Reset" clears the saved best score, highest chapter, and chapter clear counts, then shows "Scores cleared." There is no confirmation. The drop already on screen keeps its current score until that drop ends. A new launch always starts at "01" with score "0", even when "Highest chapter" is higher. No sample scores or sample runs are placed on the device, and an unfinished drop is not restored.

Best score, highest chapter, and chapter clear counts stay on this device, and "Contact" on Settings opens the support page in the browser.
