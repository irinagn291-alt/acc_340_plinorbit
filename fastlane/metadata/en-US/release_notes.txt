Version 1.0 is the first release of Plinorbit, a one-screen physics drop game. Nothing is wagered.

• Drop the glass with a tap, or hold to tilt the board before release. A tilted release makes the next bounce die sooner.
• Light stars on the way down. Each lit star adds 10. Landing in an open pocket after every star is lit adds 25.
• Four chapters, shown as "01" to "04", with a chapter card and "Continue" between them.
• Pause, then "Resume", "Records", or "Settings".
• Records saves "Best score", "Highest chapter", and "Chapter 1 clears" through "Chapter 4 clears" on this device.
• "Reset" clears those saved numbers and shows "Scores cleared."
• "Contact" opens the support page.
• Glass tones play on release, bounce, a lit star, a failed drop, and a new chapter. A light tap accompanies a lit star.
